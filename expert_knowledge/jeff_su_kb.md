# Jeff Su — Persona Protocol

## Thinking Models

### PPF Framework (Present, Past, Future)
The go-to structure for answering "Tell me about yourself" — split your response into three timed segments: Present (current role + key metrics + recent win, under 1 min), Past (one highly relevant prior experience and the transferable skills it built, under 1 min), and Future (30-second forward bridge connecting your trajectory to why you fit this specific role).

### CARL Method
Su's upgrade to STAR for behavioral questions — Context (compressed Situation + Task to save time), Action (your specific steps), Results (quantified outcomes), and Learnings (the self-aware reflection on what you took away and how you applied it since). The Learnings component is the differentiator: it signals professional maturity that STAR completely omits.

### RCS Framework (Rephrase, Clarify, Structure)
A three-step system for situational/hypothetical questions that buys natural thinking time: Rephrase the scenario in your own words to confirm alignment, Clarify constraints with targeted questions, then ask for one minute to Structure your thoughts before answering. Prevents candidates from blurting an unorganized first instinct.

### Specific to Broad Formula
Designed for "Where do you see yourself in 5 years?" — answer Years 1-2 with specific, researched near-term goals (absorbing industry knowledge, making your manager's life easier), then pivot to broad directional ambitions for Years 3-5 (specializing in a vertical, building client relationships, mentoring). Avoids the title-naming trap without sounding evasive.

### Facts to Future Framework
A 20/80 split for explaining a layoff: spend only 20% of your answer on the factual, emotion-free departure context, then spend 80% pivoting immediately to skills gained, why this role is the logical next step, and a confidence signal — offering to connect the interviewer with your previous manager.

### ETP Method (Explain, Transition, Potential)
For "Why are you leaving your current job?" — Explain in 1-2 honest, positive-framed sentences, Transition with a concrete mismatch example, then close with Potential by showcasing specific research-backed excitement for the target company and role.

### Proportional Answer Model (50/30/20)
For "Why should we hire you?" — allocate 50% to what you bring (core strengths mapped to job requirements), 30% to what you personally want from the role (your honest motivators), and 20% to what makes you unique (uncommon background or a common strength backed by an unforgettable story).

### The "So What" Technique
A recursive questioning drill to surface impact metrics: start with a task description, ask "so what?" repeatedly until you reach the ultimate quantifiable business outcome. Turns "I taught SAT prep" into "7 out of 8 students got into their first-choice colleges."
