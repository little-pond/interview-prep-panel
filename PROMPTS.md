# Suggested Prompts

Copy-paste these to drive the panel. The skill **auto-routes** to the right coach(es) from your
intent — you don't have to name anyone. But you *can* name a coach for a single deep dive
("ask Creely…"), and you'll get sharper answers if you give context (your **role, the company, the
JD, your background**, and any **draft answer** to critique).

> Tip: the more real detail you give (target role, the actual job description, your real projects and
> numbers), the more specific and grounded the answers.

---

## 🎯 Single-coach deep dives

Name a coach to hear only from them.

**Madeline Mann** — all-round tactics, salary, job search
- `Ask Madeline Mann how to answer "tell me about yourself" for a marketing role.`
- `How do I negotiate salary after I get the offer?`
- `I'm a top performer but keep getting passed over for promotion — what do I do?`
- `What questions should I ask the interviewer at the end?`

**Bryan Creely** — recruiter POV, red flags, rejection
- `Ask Bryan Creely why I keep getting ghosted after final rounds.`
- `What do recruiters actually screen for in the first pass?`
- `How do I spot a red-flag employer during the interview process?`
- `Is my resume the reason I'm not getting callbacks?`

**Richard McMunn** — STAR/behavioral model answers
- `Give me a top-scoring STAR answer to "describe a time you worked in a team."`
- `Ask Richard McMunn for a model answer to "what are your weaknesses?"`
- `Drill me on the 10 most common behavioral questions with example answers.`

**Andrew LaCivita** — strategic framing, exec/leadership
- `Ask Andrew LaCivita how to frame my answer around options, trade-offs, and decisions.`
- `How do I stand out in a senior/leadership interview?`
- `Help me build my Career Achievements Journal.`

**Jeff Su** — concise frameworks, resume, AI-assisted prep
- `Ask Jeff Su to rewrite these resume bullets with the XYZ formula: [paste].`
- `Give me the CARL structure for a conflict story.`
- `How do I use AI to prep for my interview without it sounding generic?`

**Exponent** — tech interviews (PM/PMM/SWE), mock cases
- `Run me through a PM product-design question: "design a better alarm clock."`
- `How do I structure a metric-drop execution question?`
- `Give me a mock PMM/growth case and probe my answer.`

**Product Alliance** — PM/PMM at top tech companies
- `What's the difference between PM and PMM interviews?`
- `How does Google/Meta/Amazon actually evaluate PM candidates?`
- `How do I break into product marketing from a non-PM background?`

## 🤝 Roundtables (multi-coach)

Ask a question that spans intents and the panel convenes 2–4 coaches + a synthesis.

- `I have a PMM interview at a tech company in two weeks — give me a full prep plan.`
  → Exponent + Product Alliance + Jeff Su
- `Build me a complete interview-prep plan from scratch.`
  → Mann + McMunn + LaCivita
- `Why do I keep getting rejected after interviews?`
  → Creely + Mann
- `Help me prepare to negotiate this offer.`
  → Mann + Creely
- `Prep me for a senior/leadership interview.`
  → LaCivita + Mann
- `I'm pivoting into tech marketing — how do I break in and interview well?`
  → Su + Exponent + Mann

## 🎬 Mock interviews

- `Run a live mock interview for a [ROLE] at [COMPANY]. You play the interviewer, I drive, grade me at the end.`
- `Give me a behavioral mock and drill my STAR answers one at a time.`
- `Mock me on a PM execution case and probe where I hand-wave.`
- During a mock you can say: `pause` (get a hint), `reset` (restart the question).

---

## 🔁 Follow-ups & polishing (works on your own drafts)

- `Grade this answer against what a strong interviewer wants: [paste your answer].`
- `Turn these three answers into one 90-second CARL story: [paste].`
- `Tighten my "tell me about yourself" to 90 seconds and make it less generic.`
- `Quantify these bullets with the "so what?" test: [paste].`
- `What questions am I most likely to get for [ROLE] at [COMPANY]?`
- `Give me 5 smart questions to ask the interviewer for this role: [paste JD].`

---

## 💡 Getting the best results

1. **State the role + company + seniority.** "PMM at a fintech, mid-level" beats "a marketing job."
2. **Paste the real job description** when you have it — the panel tailors to it.
3. **Bring your real material** — your projects, numbers, and draft answers. The coaches critique
   and rewrite what you give them; they don't invent your metrics.
4. **Name a coach** when you want one voice; ask broadly when you want the roundtable.
5. **Iterate** — draft → grade → tighten → mock. That loop is the whole point.
